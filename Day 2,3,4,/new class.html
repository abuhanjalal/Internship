NACTAR
NACTAR, or the National Academy for Computer Training and Research, is an important educational and training institute in Bangladesh. It provides various computer and technology-related training programs for students, teachers, and professionals. NACTAR focuses on developing practical computer skills and improving digital knowledge. Through its training and research activities, the institute contributes to the development of computer education in Bangladesh. It also helps learners build the skills they need for education, employment, and professional growth.
