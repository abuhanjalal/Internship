A website is a collection of related web pages and resources accessible through the internet under a common domain name. It can serve as a virtual space where individuals, businesses, or organizations share information, provide services, and create an online presence. Websites come in multiple types and categories, each tailored to specific purposes and consisting of essential elements contributing to their functionality and design.
